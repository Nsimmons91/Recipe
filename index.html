Instant Pot Steel Cut Chai Spiced Oatmeal

Warm up with a bowl of spiced oats!

Serving Size: 6 | Prep Time: 10 Minutes | Cook Time: 25 Minutes

Photo Credit: Honey Oatmeal

Ingredient List

  • 1 cup of steeped chi tea
  • 2 cups of water
  • 1 cup of oatmilk milk of your choice
  • 2 cups steel cut oats *do not use any other kind of oats
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon each ground cardamom and cinnamon
  • 1/8 teaspoon each ground cloves and ginger
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla bean paste or extract
  • 3 tablespoons of sweetener of choice. *adjust to taste

Cooking Directions

  1. Add all the ingredients (except vanilla bean paste) to your Instant Pot and stir.
  2. Cover and seal the pot. Cook on HIGH pressure for 4 minutes.
  3. The pressure will build for about 10 minutes, then the timer will begin.
  4. Once the timer elapses, let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes.
  5. Vent, then carefully open the lid. Add your vanilla and stir. The oats will look a bit liquidy at first, but will thicken as they cool.
